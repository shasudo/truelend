Ask any bank what CIBIL score you need and you'll hear "650 and above." Ask what score gets you
their _advertised_ rate — the one on the billboard — and the honest answer is almost always
**750 or higher**.

## The two prices of the same loan

Most lenders in India now use risk-based pricing. The same home loan, from the same bank, on the
same day can be priced half a percent apart purely on the score band. On a ₹50 lakh loan over
20 years, that half percent is roughly **₹3.5 lakh of extra interest** — the price of being at
720 instead of 755.

Below 700, something worse than pricing kicks in: filters. Many lenders' systems reject files
before a human ever reads them. The rejection itself gets recorded as an enquiry, nudging the
score further down. It's the one place in lending where failure genuinely compounds.

## What actually moves the score

CIBIL doesn't publish its exact weights, but across thousands of files the pattern is stable:

1. **Repayment history (~35%).** One EMI bounce hurts more than anything else on this list —
   and stays visible for 36 months.
2. **Utilisation (~30%).** Credit card balances above 30% of the limit read as stress, even if
   you pay in full. Above 80%, they read as distress.
3. **Age of credit (~15%).** Your oldest account is an asset. Closing an old card to "clean up"
   usually lowers the score.
4. **Mix and enquiries (~20%).** A blend of secured and unsecured credit helps; a burst of loan
   applications in one month does not.

## A realistic six-month plan

- **Month 1:** Pull your report (checking your own score is a "soft" enquiry — it never hurts).
  Dispute every error; wrong entries are more common than most people expect.
- **Months 1–6:** Zero misses. Set every EMI and card bill to auto-debit with a buffer.
- **Months 2–3:** Get card utilisation under 30% — ask for a limit increase rather than
  spending less if cash flow is tight; the ratio improves either way.
- **Months 3–6:** No new applications. Let enquiries age out of the recent window.

Six clean months typically lift a low-700s score into the 750s. It is unglamorous, and it works.
